patterns and problems with non-biological niche filling

November 20, 2026

Big biological processes—maybe unsurprisingly—map onto all sorts of social/cultural phenomena. The mechanistic ideas underlying natural selection have been widely transported into cultural studies. Statistical tools to look at genomic evolution have been rejiggered to undertand how music changes over time. Linguistics as a field has long adopted models of evolution, as reviewed here, here, and here (not, by any means, exhaustive).
